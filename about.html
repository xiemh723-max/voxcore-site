我们是谁Who We Are

芯片、算法、整机，都自己做Chips, Algorithms and Devices,All In-House

2018 年，我们从一段声学算法起家，发现市场上几乎没有为「录音 + 转写」而生的芯片。于是我们从算法做到芯片，从芯片做到模组，从模组做到整机——三层产品全部自研自造。正因如此，从一颗芯片到一台录音笔，声音质量与转写准确率都在我们手里可控。In 2018 we started from an acoustic algorithm and found almost no chip built for "record + transcribe." So we went from algorithm to chip, chip to module, module to device — all self-developed, self-built. That's why, from a single die to a finished recorder, audio quality and ASR accuracy stay under our control end to end.
